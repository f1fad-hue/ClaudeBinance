Rationale

Horizon first. Over ten years cash drag compounds: SGOV turns $10,000 into $14,506, QQQ into $24,805. The macro signal chooses which equities, not whether.
Tilt to the cheaper bloc. Asia / Emerging ranks first at every horizon and IEMG trades at 11.5×; per unit of risk it is level with QQQ (0.274 vs 0.272) while earning less (8.73% vs 9.51%).
Size to normalized volatility. The weights are set against −33.8% drawdown, not today’s −25.9%.
SGOV at 30% is a drawdown budget. Scaling a fixed risky mix against cash holds return-per-drawdown at 0.1107 whatever the cash weight, so no optimizer can pick it.
BMNR capped at 5%. 24.3% of risk from 5% of capital; total loss costs 5%.
Where the model was overruled. The best return-per-risk book, QQQ 40 / IEMG 50 / SGOV 5 / BMNR 5 (Sharpe 0.260 vs 0.246), holds a 5% reserve. Rejected.
Recommendation. Hold QQQ 35 / IEMG 30 / SGOV 30 / BMNR 5: −33.8% normalized drawdown against the baseline’s −36.4%, for 0.32 points of CAGR. Rebalance semi-annually or on a 5-point drift.
